A game of singing upward

Hollowsong

You are a seed of blown glass at the bottom of Hollowdeep, a cave whose crystals still remember their notes. The Hush is rising from below, and whatever it reaches goes quiet for good. Sing, and climb.

    Fingers rest on A S D F, thumb on the space bar.

    Sing
    Hold a key to sing its note. The nearest crystal of that colour within reach pulls you toward it.
    Chords
    Hold two or three notes and you are pulled several ways at once. Chords are how you steer.
    Breath
    Singing spends breath. Going quiet refills it, but you start to fall.
    Wear
    A crystal cracks while you lean on it, and soon shatters. Keep moving.
    Danger
    Red shards crack your glass; three cracks and you break. Pale moths follow any song and drink your breath. Go quiet to lose them.
    Echoes
    Gold echoes refill your breath and push the Hush back down.
